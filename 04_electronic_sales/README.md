# Electronic Sales Customer Behaviour Analytics
Applied advanced econometric modelling (PSM, multinomial logistic regression, and 2SLS) to evaluate loyalty programme effectiveness, customer ratings, and product-level price elasticity using transactional retail data. Identified zero causal impact of loyalty membership on spending, quantified category-level pricing sensitivity, and generated strategic recommendations for incentive redesign, targeted pricing, and customer value optimisation.
