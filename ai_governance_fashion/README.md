# Shein, LATR & Algorithmic Externalities: AI Governance in Fast Fashion
Analysed Shein’s LATR real-time supply chain model as a data-driven optimisation system balancing micro-batch production, inventory minimisation, and commercial scale. Identified governance, sustainability, and environmental risks from algorithmic over-optimisation, and developed mitigation strategies including human-in-the-loop governance, sustainable sourcing targets, and redesigned analytics frameworks to reduce waste and emissions.
